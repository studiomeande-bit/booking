FAQ

The essentials, up front.

How long does a pre-wedding shoot take?

It depends on scope, usually with location changes. We agree on route and time when you book.

What about the dress or outfit?

Your own dress, suit or casual are all fine. We share outfit tips that suit your concept in advance.

Do you also shoot on the wedding day?

Yes, wedding-day and reception coverage are available on request. We quote after checking the flow and scope.

What happens if it rains?

If it rains, we offer to reschedule or an indoor alternative, and reach out in good time depending on the weather.

Can we communicate in English?

Yes. We guide you in English, German and Korean — from booking through to delivery.
