The question everyone asks

Do German applications
still need a photo?

Since the General Equal Treatment Act (AGG) the photo is voluntary — nobody has to include one. In practice many employers still expect it, especially with the classic German CV templates. If you decide to include one, it should be calm, current and suited to the role you are applying for. That is what we work on during the appointment.

Getting ready

Clothes, background,
expression.

01

Clothes

Wear what you would wear to the interview. Quiet colours and minimal pattern read more cleanly on screen. A second top in your bag never hurts.

02

Background

Light and neutral for classic applications, darker for business and artist profiles. Two backgrounds are included from Profil Business upwards.

03

Expression

No forced smile. We guide you with a few quiet cues — for most people that takes only a couple of minutes.

04

Glasses & hair

Glasses are fine, we watch for reflections. Come with your own hair and make-up. If you would like help, we can put you in touch with a hair-and-make-up partner — she charges for that separately.

FAQ

The essentials, up front.

Is a photo required on a German application?

No. Since the General Equal Treatment Act (AGG) the photo is voluntary — nobody has to include one. In practice many employers still expect it, especially with the classic German CV templates.

What does an application photo cost here?

Profil Basic €55, Profil Business €95, Profil Professional €130 — each including 19% VAT. The difference is shooting time, the number of retouched images, backgrounds and outfits.

How long does the appointment take?

About 15 minutes to settle in, plus the shooting time: 15 minutes for Basic, 30 minutes for Business, 60 minutes for Professional.

What should I wear?

Whatever you would wear to the interview. Quiet colours, minimal pattern. Bring a second top if you like and we compare them on the monitor.

When do I get the photos?

You get every frame through the cloud to choose from. The retouched images usually arrive within 2 to 3 weeks, prints by post 2 to 4 working days after dispatch. If you have a deadline, tell us beforehand.

May I use the photos on LinkedIn and XING?

Yes, for your application and your own profiles. If a company uses the picture, for example on its website or in job ads, we agree that in writing beforehand.

Is an AI portrait an alternative?

We work only with real photographs. An AI image often stops looking like you on second glance — and that is exactly what shows up in the interview.

Which language is the session in?

English, German or Korean, whichever you prefer.

When can I come?

Tue–Fri 9:30am–1pm and 3:30pm–6pm, Sat from 9am to 4pm, by appointment. The studio is closed on Sundays, Mondays and public holidays.
