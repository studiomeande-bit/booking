E-Passbild

How the German
E-Passbild works.

For German ID cards, passports and residence permits we transmit your photo digitally. You receive a printout with a QR code. Hand that printout in at the office; they scan the code and retrieve the photo.

The Korean passport still takes paper photos: 35 × 45 mm, white background, eight prints, included in the price.

FAQ

The essentials, up front.

Does it meet the biometric spec?

Yes. We shoot to the German E-Passbild (biometric) standard and other specs. The Korean passport format is also possible.

How long does it take?

The shot itself takes a few minutes. After the spec check we hand over the digital file immediately.

Can you do a baby passport photo?

Yes, baby and child passport photos are possible. We adjust background and posture to the spec.

Do I get a print too?

That depends on the country. The Korean passport includes 8 prints in the price. For the German E-Passbild you receive the printout with the QR code for the office. For other countries, prints follow that country's requirement.

Do I need to book?

Yes, we work by appointment, so there is no waiting. You can book online, including for the same day.

How much does a passport photo cost?

€30 for one country, including 19% VAT. Each additional country in the same appointment is €5 extra.

Do you take US visa photos?

Yes. US passport and visa photos are 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm) on a white background. You receive prints and a digital file you can upload to the online application.

I need a photo for my German residence permit. Is that the E-Passbild?

Yes. For German ID cards, passports and residence permits we transmit the photo digitally and give you a printout with a QR code. You hand that printout in at the office.

Can I get photos for two countries in one visit?

Yes. Choose the countries on the booking page. We take the photos once and prepare each one to its own spec. Each additional country is €5.

Where can I park?

The studio has no dedicated parking. City Parkhaus, Parkhaus Altstadt and Rathausparkplatz are nearby.
