FAQ

The essentials, up front.

How long does a Frankfurt photo shoot take?

An outdoor shoot usually takes 30 to 60 minutes. We agree on the route and number of locations with you when you book.

How many edited photos do I get?

It depends on the package. You receive all originals via Google Cloud, and the selected frames are colour-graded. Full details are on the booking page.

I'm travelling solo — can I still book?

Yes, we photograph solo travellers often. We capture natural portraits against Frankfurt's streets and landmarks.

What happens if it rains?

If it rains, we offer to reschedule or move to an indoor alternative. After booking we reach out in good time depending on the weather.

Can we communicate in English?

Yes. We guide you in English, German and Korean — from booking through to delivery.
