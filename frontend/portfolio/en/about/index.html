Studio mean · About

A studio built for quiet portrait work and reliable business communication.

Studio mean works from Oberursel near Frankfurt and combines editorial portraiture with family sessions, weddings, business events and video assignments for local, international and Korean clients in Germany.

Region Oberursel · Frankfurt · Rhine-Main · Germany

Private Clients

Portrait, family and studio sessions

For families, couples and individual portrait clients, Studio mean keeps the experience calm, clear and image-focused. Sessions are designed around expression, posture, rhythm and a restrained editorial sensibility.

B2B

Corporate events and brand content

For companies, agencies and teams in Germany, the work includes business portraits, executive headshots, event coverage, conferences, interviews and short brand videos. More corporate and event projects will continue to be added to the portfolio.

Korean Business Support

A local partner for Korean teams in Germany

Studio mean also serves as a local contact point for Korean companies, agencies and visiting teams that need photography or video production in Germany. Project communication can happen in Korean, English or German.

Production

Photo and video in one flow

Alongside photography, Studio mean can support interview setups, brand clips, short-form event documentation and visual content that sits naturally beside still photography in the same production flow.
