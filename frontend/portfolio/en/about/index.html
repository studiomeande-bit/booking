Studio mean · About

Calm visual work with a clear production process.

Studio mean works from Oberursel near Frankfurt and combines restrained portrait photography with family sessions, weddings, corporate events and film projects for local and international clients in Germany.

Private Clients

Portraits, families and personal stories

For families, couples and individual portrait clients, the experience stays calm, clear and image-focused. Sessions are shaped around expression, posture, timing and a restrained editorial sensibility.

B2B

Corporate events and brand content

For companies, agencies and teams in Germany, the work includes business portraits, executive headshots, event coverage, conferences, interviews and short brand films. The production stays clear even when the schedule is full.

International Production

Local production support in Germany

Studio mean also works as a local production partner for companies, agencies and visiting teams that need photography or film content in Germany. Briefing, planning and on-site coordination can happen in English, German or Korean.

Production

Photography and film in one flow

Alongside photography, Studio mean can support interview setups, brand clips, short event films and visual content that sits naturally beside still photography in the same production flow.
